Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

At Chat Ads Co., we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide when using our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our services, you may provide business information, account information, advertising information, and payment or service-related details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Any information provided to us is used only for the purpose of providing, operating, and improving our services, including setting up and managing advertising campaigns, communicating with clients, and providing customer support.

3. We Do Not Store Business Information

We do not permanently store your business information or advertising credentials. Information provided to us is used only as necessary to provide the requested service.

Once the information is no longer required for providing the service, it is not retained by us, subject to any information that may be required to be retained by law.

4. Credentials & Access

If you provide login credentials, advertising account access, API keys, or similar credentials, these are used solely for the purpose of providing the requested service.

We do not sell, rent, or use your credentials for purposes unrelated to your service.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your business information or credentials to third parties.

Information may only be shared with service providers or platforms when necessary to deliver the service you have requested, or when required by law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information provided to us from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to ensure the website functions properly and to understand website usage. Where applicable, you may control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Third-Party Services

Our services may interact with third-party platforms, advertising services, payment providers, or other tools. These third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing their policies when using their services.

9. Your Rights

You may contact us to ask what information we hold about you, request correction or deletion of information, or ask questions about how your information is used.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Chat Ads Co. through the contact details provided on our website.
